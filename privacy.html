Privacy, in plain words.

This site is simple on purpose. Here is exactly what happens to what you tell us, and what you can ask us to do with it.

  • The form on this site does not store what you type.
  • Nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp.
  • No advertising pixels are active on this site right now.

Who we are

Stacked Marketing runs this site, stackedmarketing.co.za. We are responsible for the information you send us.

To ask us anything about your information, message us on WhatsApp at 062 177 9799.

When you fill in the form

The form does not store what you type. It is not saved on this site and it is not sent to us.

Your browser uses your answers to write a WhatsApp message, then opens WhatsApp with that message ready. The message, with your name and business name, is passed to WhatsApp in the link that opens it. That happens before you press send.

Nothing reaches Stacked until you press send in WhatsApp. If you close WhatsApp without sending, we never see it.

Once you press send

When your message arrives, we hold:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • what you told us
  • your WhatsApp number

We use it to reply to you and to set up the work you ask for.

Advertising pixels

No advertising pixels are active on this site right now.

If we switch on the Meta Pixel, we will update this page first.

Your rights under POPIA

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you a say over your information. You can:

  • ask us what we hold about you
  • ask us to correct it
  • ask us to delete it

Send your request to us on WhatsApp at 062 177 9799. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Other services this site uses

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

WhatsApp is run by Meta. What WhatsApp receives, including the message in the link from our form, is covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.
